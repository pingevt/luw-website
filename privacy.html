Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

This policy covers the website at littleurchinworkshop.com and any apps published by Little Urchin Workshop LLC ("Little Urchin Workshop"). The short version: as little data as possible is collected, and none of it is sold.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics, ad trackers, or forms.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operations, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives standard request data under Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you email Little Urchin Workshop, your address and message are used only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. They are never sold or shared for marketing.

Apps

Apps published by Little Urchin Workshop collect only the data they need to work. Any data an app does collect is described in its App Store privacy details, and it is not sold or used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
